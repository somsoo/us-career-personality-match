    🧮 How the scoring works

    • Scores: each of the six sections has 10 activities, and your score for an interest area is the number you checked, from 0 to 10.
    • Top three: your highest score is your primary interest, followed by your second and third. The official instructions say: “If there are ties, choose the interest with activities that you think are the best fit for you.” The page asks you to choose whenever a tie affects your top three. Areas with no checks are not ranked.
    • Careers: for your chosen interest and Job Zone, the page shows the matching group from the official career listings, which are a sample of careers linked with each interest and Job Zone. Careers marked ** or *** were placed in the group by their second or third highest interest.
    • Learn more: the official materials suggest searching any career title on My Next Move (mynextmove.org), a free U.S. Department of Labor site, for its tasks, skills, education, pay and outlook.

    ❓ Frequently asked questions

    What does this test measure?

    It measures six types of work interests: Realistic, Investigative, Artistic, Social, Enterprising and Conventional. It uses the 60 work activities of the O*NET® Interest Profiler Short Form from the U.S. Department of Labor, and your score in each area is the number of its 10 activities you checked.

    Is this a personality test?

    No. It asks which work activities you would like to do, which is about interests, not personality or ability. It does not use any personality type system, and the results are not a diagnosis or a prediction of how well you would do in a job.

    What if two interest areas are tied?

    The official instructions say that if there are ties, you choose the interest with activities that you think are the best fit for you. So when a tie affects your top three, the page asks you to choose instead of deciding for you. Areas with no checks are not ranked.

    What is a Job Zone?

    A Job Zone is a level of education, training and experience that careers need, from Job Zone 1, little or no preparation, to Job Zone 5, extensive preparation. Pick the Job Zone that matches what you have now, and optionally a higher one you plan to work toward, to see careers at each level.

    Are my answers saved or shared?

    Your checks and choices are saved only in this browser so you can come back to them. They are never sent to us. A share link contains only your interest code and Job Zones, not your answers. Use Clear answers to delete them.

    ⚠️ Please note

    This is a career exploration tool, not career counseling or a hiring test. Interests are one part of choosing a career; skills, values, pay, job outlook and where you live matter too. The career lists are a sample, not a complete list. For personal guidance, talk with a school counselor or a career counselor.
